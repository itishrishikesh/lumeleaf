# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, accept responsibility, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, sexualized attention, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this code. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and respond proportionately.

This document is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
